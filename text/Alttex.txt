Introduction

Digital technologies are advancing day by day, enhancing our quality of life at the same time. As information exchange processes accelerate, the volumes of transmitted data increase. The advancement of technology allows us to communicate, exchange information, make purchases, trade between countries and continents without having to leave our home. These changes can not be ignored by the financial sector, of which the main element is money. Historically, this important element of payment had many changes. At first, it was the exchange of natural products, later - coins, then - paper money. Now it's electronic or digital money.

In 2009, the first crypto currency appeared, Bitcoin. It is a digital currency which is encoded cryptographically; its cost depends entirely on the supply and demand of the market. Cryptocurrency allows transactions to be conducted between users without intermediaries (banks, government, etc.). After the successful appearance of Bitcoin, many other crypto-currencies appeared one after another. Eg: Ethereum, Lisk, EOS, Waves, Civic, 0x, Ripple. Cryptocurrency wallets are used to store these funds. Eg: Airbitz, Armory, Bitgo, Blockchain, Coinbase, Greenaddress, Ledger, Jaxx, Mycelium, Samourai, Xapo and others. The exact number of active users of cryptocurrency cannot be determined. However, through obtaining statistical data through surveys and analysis conducted by several companies. The results showed that in 2013, the number of crypto wallets amounted 8.2 million, and in 2016 - 35 million. Thus, showing that cryptocurrency has consolidated its place among money resources and has a tendency to develop even further.

All crypto exchanges can be divided into two types: centralized and decentralized. A centralized exchange is an exchange where all data and information are stored in one place. The fact that these exchanges, in contrast to large banks, don't have the opportunity to invest a considerable amount of money in improving security, causes such exchanges to often fall under hackers' attacks, fraud from internal employees and price manipulation. On a decentralized exchange - directly to the person who engages in illegal activities. Therefore, decentralized exchanges are unattractive for frauds. In cases of detection with transactions that are contrary to the law, law bodies have the right and the opportunity to close a centralized exchange. As for decentralized exchanges, the trade is peer-to-peer. In order to stop such trade, it is necessary to interrupt the actions of a particular person by withdrawing his software.

Features and benefits of the platform

Blockchain technology. First, let's get acquainted with the technology of Blockchain. What does this technology represent? Blockchain is a kind of aggregate of certain records that are included in blocks. These blocks are connected with each other by means of cryptographic verification. Blockchain is a digital safe that stores the truth, which is agreed with all network members. These records are stored in di erent places. The registry they create is absolutely decentralized. The software, which uses blocking technology, based on open source code, is called DApps and does not have distinctive features that can be characterized by it. This is decentralized (the register of records is stored in a decentralized detachment), openness (changes should be made by the voice of the majority of users or by reaching a compromise), encouragement and stimulation (blockade validators need to be rewarded by giving them crypto currency), PoW (Proof of work), PoS (Proof of Stake), which demonstrates the proof of value.

30



Decentralized technology blocking allows you to make transac-tions on the stock exchange instant. The decentralized network operates on the principle of P2P, "from user-to-user". Due to the fact that the information is not stored centrally, there are no vulnerability points that attract hackers. To increase the level of security, personal and public keys are used, which is encryption.

Decentralization. The basis for the Alttex platform is a decentralized network where each user is part of Alttex's blockchain. The full responsibility for carrying out the transaction is assigned to the Alttex network block, while all reporting on the transactions is stored in encrypted user, same as bitcoin. To increase the transaction speed, the Altx token uses the DPoS consensus algorithm. This means that participants transfer responsibility for verifying a particular transaction to selected delegates (nodes with the largest capital). This is the basis of the maximum transaction speed. The voice that the candidate receives in order to become a miner is very flexible: it is easily assigned for fulfilling the requirements and can also be easily taken away in case of an error. Miners receive a financial reward for processing the transaction, so they are interested in making sure that reasonable transactions are included in the blocks.

31



In the opposite cases, users can withdraw their votes and the miner will drop out of the list of block producers. Thus, for elected delegates, there is no need to wait for the verification of all nodes of the network if the block to which the transaction fits comes from a trusted delegate. This scheme gives a number of unshakable advantages: improbable speed development and excellent controllability. DPOS network as of the current time has never been hacked.
