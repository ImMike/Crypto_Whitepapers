Introduction

Mondo.one - a freelance Stock Exchange, a searching machine for contacts around the world, arbitrage of trades and an open rating system of contractors on Blockchain.

Mondo  in the Esperanto word for "world".

We have an idea of Mondo in January 2017. Our team has been working in the field of freelancing for many years and we often encountered both with unscrupulous contractors and customers. Finally, we launched Telegram Bot named "RateMe" - rating platform for customers (we were inspired by idea come from popular tv-series "Black mirror"). Follow this link to check it out! t.me/ratemebot

This is the idea of uniting the world and erasing any virtual boundaries. Imagine, you can find any contact you need, regardless what country are you in now. Our service helps you to do it as fast as possible with ongoing support from trustworthy experts. Global searching created not only for looking for freelancers, that's why we will provide expanded search capabilities so you can find any person you need.

During the recovery after an economic crisis a lot of citizens are being offered with less gainful jobs without guarantee of stable placement. It's no secret that freelance is the modern solution of unemployment and the future of universal and effective work.

However, nowadays not many platforms are trying to help contractors by minimizing the commission to withdraw funds from the exchange or, for instance, by automatic daily job search and, that's more important, by trust.

A large number of people do not know how to enter the international markets (Asia, Europe, USA etc). Contact exchange process erases scopes between



countries and their unique characteristics so you will be able to find person, who is ready to help you, wherever he is. We unite the world.

Problems of analogues

Our main goal - solve problems of other freelance exchanges such as: Large commission of withdrawal of funds from the exchange for

contractors. Mistrust on both sides. Non-transparent rating system. Limited markets, language barriers. Only freelance-oriented trades. An outdated and inconvenient verification system.

Large commission to withdraw funds (freelance stock exchange UpWork)

Choose UpWork as an example. There is limited list for searching. There is no digest for the convenience of users - you need to search and check new offers quite often.



Solution

Transparent rating system based on Blockchain 1 person = 1 account. Average score and public reviews of each contractor

Escrow solves the problem of mistrust between the contractor and the customer, helps to avoid conflicts. Arbitrage of transactions.

Search for any request, online contractors or offline - no borders, no barriers.

Minimization of commissions on the side of the service. Earnings for token holders, savings for active users
