ICO
   (Initial
   Coin
   Offering)
   is
   a

   technological

   and
   start--ups

   emission
   and

   new
   crypto--tokens

   investors.
   Unlike
   IPO
   investors,
   cryptocurrency
   investors
   do
   not
   receive
   a
   stake
   in
   the
   company
   and
   cannot

   any

   influence
   the
   internal
   management
   decisions.
   In
   fact,
   ICO
   is
   another

  that

  them

   use
  the
  product

  more
  favorable
  terms.
  
   
   Thanks

  fundraising

  cryptocurrency,

   that
   project

   their

  viable.
  Tight
  deadlines
  and
  fast

  of
  fundraising

  do

  to
  perform
  due

  before
  investing.
  People
  invest

  the
  maximum

   proper
   analysis,
   hoping
   to
   buy
   the
   shares
   of
   another
   `APPLE'

   'GOOGLE`.
   
   Blockchain
   technology
   could
   really

   to

   multibillion--dollar

  only

   not

   the

   time,

   guaranteed,

   ICO
   funds
   are
   already
   in
   their
   hands.
   Thus,
   this
   innovative
   fundraising
   approach

2.
  What
  is
  Escrow
  

Escrow
   account
   is
   a
   dedicated
   account
   which
   keeps
   assets,
   documents
   or
   cash
   until
   occurrence
  of
  circumstances
  or
  until
  specified
  obligations
  are
  implemented.
  
   The
  essence
  of
  escrow
  is
  when
  buyers
  and
  sellers
  use
  a
  `third
  trusted
  party'
  to
  hold
  funds
   until
  all
  parties
  fulfill
  their
  obligations.
  Thus,
  an
  escrow
  has
  a
  single
  purpose
  --
  to
  guarantee
   implementation
   of
   these
   obligations.
   Consequently,
   the
   number
   of
   claims
   for
   transactions
   with
   escrow
   is
   negligible,
   since
   each
   of
   the
   parties
   is
   interested
   in
   fulfilling
   the
   conditions
   which
  were
  stipulated
  in
  advance.
   When
   payments
   are
   made
   through
   the
   escrow
   account,
   escrow
   agent
   transfers
   the
   funds
   from
   account
   to
   one
   party
   only
   if
   this
   party
   has
   fulfilled
   its
   obligations
   to
   another
   party.
   Therefore,
  escrow
  account
  allows
  parties
  to
  ensure
  fulfillment
  of
  obligations
  and
  minimize
   the
  risk
  of
  fraud
  by
  one
  of
  the
  parties.
   With
  regards
  to
  ICO
  Escrow
  market,
  `multisig'
  is
  an
  effective
  solution
  for
  increased
  security
   of
  collection,
  storage
  and
  distribution
  of
  funds
  received
  during
  ICO
  /
  TGE.

   `escrow'

   not
   really
   fit
   the

   in
   traditional

  lack
  of

  be

  for
  goods
  or

   (beneficiary)

   fulfilling
   his
   obligations

   to
   agreement
   only
   after
   the
   buyer
   (depositor)
   transfers
   amount
   of
   funds
   stipulated
   in
   the
   contract.
   The
   lack
   of
   legislative
   qualification
   of
   ICO
   fundraising
   does
   not
   allow

   accurately
   determine
   the

   relationship
