Concept

Platform to monetize your knowledge. The objective is that in less than 5 minutes and in 3 simple steps, you can have your knowledge monetizable in a site using our platform KNOWS.Online



4

Problem

Currently, there are many platforms where you can upload your courses but in the cryptocurrencies world, does not yet exist something agile and fast that make it easy to sell content of value.



5

Solution

With KNOWS.Online you will be able to monetize your knowledge in 5 minutes. Once you have your PDF ready, just upload it and prepare for other users to buy it in the market and also to promote and earn commissions if they see that it is useful and interesting.

Steps to upload a product:

1. Language (English, Spanish, others), Title, and Description of your digital product.

2. Price and warranty *

3. Commission for people who want to promote your product **

* The platform does not charge commission for publishing the product. Only retains a 10% during 6 months for possible returns and charges a small 5% withdrawal fee.

** The commission is limitless. Like more promotion, the more commission.
