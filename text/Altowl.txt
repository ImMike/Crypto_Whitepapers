4. Development of backend at scale

At this stage, we plan to develop a scalable de-centralized multi-server (cloud) backend to ensure solution to following tasks: Storage of a huge amount of text information (there is a lot of pages on the Internet. By adding just one short comment to each page, we get the huge amount of information. The new backend should provide the ability to store the constantly increasing array of information. Quick access to data -- with a large number of concurrent requests. Data updating maintaining their integrity. Fault tolerance, where the fault of a single server will not result in the failure of the entire data warehouse, as well as to violation of their integrity.
